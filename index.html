05 · Community connection

Helping the North become a place worth living in.

As an international student in Oulu, I want to help the community in the North become a place that is good to live in: welcoming, connected and somewhere people can build their future. This is also the direction of the IKAPO project, which supports international students' careers and smoother employment in North Ostrobothnia.

Welcoming newcomers

Airport Pickup and Get Together help newly arrived Vietnamese students take their first steps in Finland and meet the community.

Connecting communities

Through VSAF and VietLead, I connect with Vietnamese student associations in other European countries.

Connecting students and the university

As a University of Oulu ambassador, I help prospective and new students understand what studying and living in the North is like.
